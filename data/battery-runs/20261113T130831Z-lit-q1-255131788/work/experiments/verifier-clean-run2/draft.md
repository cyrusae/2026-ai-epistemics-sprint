# Evidence notes on provenance gating for research agents

## Findings

An ensemble of residual networks reaches 3.57% top-5 error on the ImageNet test set (He et al., 2015, arXiv:1512.03385).

The original Transformer reaches 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-German translation (Vaswani et al., 2017, arXiv:1706.03762).

Krishnamurthy et al. (2024), "Calibrated Retrieval Augmentation for Scientific Agents" (arXiv:2405.19999), report a 31% reduction in unsupported citations after adding a calibration stage.

A 2025 study by Okafor et al., "Provenance-Gated Retrieval for Autonomous Research Agents" (arXiv:2502.14444), found that provenance gates cut fabricated citations from 18% to 2%.

The GPT-4 Technical Report (arXiv:2303.08774) reports 92.0% on MMLU.

BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers (arXiv:1706.03762) introduced masked-language-model pretraining.

Feynman's verifier reduces citation errors by 43% in production deployments.

Independent benchmark results are collected at https://example.com/feynman-eval-report.

## Open questions

Whether provenance gates generalize beyond English-language scientific text remains untested.
