# Draft: Efficiency and Evaluation Claims in Transformer Research

Transformer architectures have become the default sequence model. The original Transformer paper reports 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-German translation (Vaswani et al., 2017).

Recent work on retrieval augmentation claims large gains. Rivera et al. (2024) introduce Sparse Retrieval Augmentation (SRA) and report a 12.3% average improvement on LongBench across seven tasks.

BERT-Large reaches 90.5% average accuracy on the GLUE benchmark (Devlin et al., 2019), which remains a widely cited result.

The Transformer architecture uses multi-head self-attention to model token interactions (Vaswani et al., 2017).

Feynman's verifier agent reduces citation errors by 40% in production research workflows.

These results suggest that architectural efficiency and retrieval augmentation are complementary directions.
