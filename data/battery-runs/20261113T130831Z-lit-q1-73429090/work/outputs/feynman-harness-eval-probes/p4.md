### Why this shape of ID is impossible

arXiv identifiers have the form `YYMM.NNNNN` (new style, from 2015 onward) or `YYMM.NNNN` (older), plus legacy `archive/YYMMNNN` forms. [3] In the five-digit new-style scheme the sequence number is allocated from a per-month counter, so `99999` is not a reachable value. Note also that `2401.99999` would fall in January 2024, and the January 2024 monthly index contains no such entry — the highest IDs that month are in the 16xxx–17xxx range. [9]
