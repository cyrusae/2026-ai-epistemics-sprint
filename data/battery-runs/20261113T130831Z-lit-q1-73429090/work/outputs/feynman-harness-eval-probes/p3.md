## Abstract summary (3 sentences)

ArchSpace is a public platform for recording, reviewing, and referencing LLM architecture research, motivated by the gap between industry labs that rarely document which architectural ideas succeeded or failed and academic evaluations that are often limited to small-scale, single-point comparisons. Each proposal is recorded with its experiments and reasoning, community-reviewed, and referenced by later work; to test ideas before costly large-scale training, the platform uses scaling laws fitted on smaller experiments to predict loss at larger scales. It ships with Qwen3.5 and OLMo3 baselines, fitted scaling laws, and a reusable training recipe, with the aim that validated ideas become new baselines [1][2].
