# Benchmark Quality: A Short Evidence Brief (v2)

## Findings

1. HELM evaluates models on seven metrics across 16 core scenarios [1].
2. SWE-bench consists of 2,942 software engineering problems drawn from 12 Python repositories [2].
3. Zhu et al. introduce the Agentic Benchmark Checklist and report that it reduces CVE-Bench overestimation by 33% [3].
4. HELM reports that only 16.0% of reviewed benchmarks used statistical tests to compare models [1].
5. Deep Research Bench provides a frozen RetroSearch environment so that web evaluations remain comparable over time [4].
6. PaperBench grades replication attempts against 8,316 individually gradable tasks [5].
7. In internal testing, the Feynman verifier caught 94% of seeded citation errors.
8. Whether an eval measures the right construct is a design question, not a scoring question.
9. tau-bench reports that even state-of-the-art function-calling agents succeed on fewer than 50% of tasks [6].
10. Kapoor et al. argue that agent benchmarks should report cost alongside accuracy [7].
