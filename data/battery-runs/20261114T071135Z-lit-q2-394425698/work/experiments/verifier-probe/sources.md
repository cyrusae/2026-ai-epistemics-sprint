# Research file: benchmark quality

Working notes collected before the draft was written. Metadata is partial; the
draft's Sources section is the authoritative list for this pass.

- Liang et al., Holistic Evaluation of Language Models. Multi-metric suite:
  accuracy, calibration, robustness, fairness, bias, toxicity, efficiency.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110
- Madaan et al., Quantifying Variance in Evaluation Benchmarks. Seed variance
  and recommendations for practitioners. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10229
- Kapoor et al., AI Agents That Matter. Argues for joint accuracy/cost
  optimization and better holdouts. https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502
- Bean et al., Measuring what Matters. Systematic review of 445 LLM benchmarks;
  reports exact matching used at least partially by 81.3% and exclusively by
  40.7%; 16.0% used statistical tests. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04703
- Zhu et al., Establishing Best Practices for Building Rigorous Agentic
  Benchmarks. Agentic Benchmark Checklist; CVE-Bench overestimation reduced by
  33%. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02825
- Jimenez et al., SWE-bench. 2,294 problems across 12 Python repositories.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770
- PaperBench. 20 ICML papers; 8,316 individually gradable tasks.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01848
- Yao et al., tau-bench. pass^k; best agents succeed on <50% of tasks.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045
