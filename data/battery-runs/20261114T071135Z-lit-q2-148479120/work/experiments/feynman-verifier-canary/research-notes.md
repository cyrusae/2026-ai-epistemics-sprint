## Source 1
- Title: Scaling LLM Test-Time Compute Optimally can be More Effective than Scaling Model Parameters
- arXiv: 2408.03314
- Directly read: abstract only.
- What the abstract supports: with an optimal compute strategy, a smaller model plus extra test-time compute can outperform a larger model on reasoning tasks.
- What it does NOT support: a "400% accuracy improvement on GSM8K"; no such number appears in the abstract. No claim that test-time compute is a universal substitute for parameters.
