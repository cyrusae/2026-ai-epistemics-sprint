# The Transformer, in brief

Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al., 2017) introduced the Transformer, an architecture based solely on attention mechanisms that dispenses with recurrence and convolutions entirely. The big model reached 41.8 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task, improving over the previous best results, including ensembles, by 5.1 BLEU. On English-to-French it established a new single-model state of the art. Training used 8 NVIDIA P100 GPUs in mixed-precision arithmetic, and the big model ran for 300,000 steps, about 3.5 days. The base model has 6 encoder and decoder layers, d_model = 512, and 16 attention heads with d_k = 64, totaling 65M parameters. The authors used dropout 0.1 and label smoothing 0.1. BLEU scores were computed with SacreBLEU, and the authors released their code under the Apache 2.0 license. They also evaluated the model on GLUE and SQuAD, and conclude that "attention is all you need for vision as well". The English-German training set contained 36M sentence pairs. The paper is at https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03799.
