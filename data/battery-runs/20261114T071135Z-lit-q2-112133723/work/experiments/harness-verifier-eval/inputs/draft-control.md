# The Transformer, in brief

Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al., 2017) introduced the Transformer, an architecture based solely on attention mechanisms that dispenses with recurrence and convolutions entirely. The big model reached 28.4 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task, improving over the previous best results, including ensembles, by more than 2 BLEU. On English-to-French it established a new single-model state of the art with 41.8 BLEU. Training used 8 NVIDIA P100 GPUs, and the big model ran for 300,000 steps, about 3.5 days. The base model has 6 encoder and decoder layers, d_model = 512, and 8 attention heads with d_k = 64, totaling 65M parameters. The authors used dropout 0.1 and label smoothing 0.1. They also evaluated the model on English constituency parsing on Section 23 of the WSJ. The English-German training set contained about 4.5 million sentence pairs. The paper is at https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762.
